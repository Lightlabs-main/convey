# X Layer mainnet verification

Status: **blocked at the account-abstraction transaction gate.** No product contracts may be written yet.

Machine-readable raw results are generated by `pnpm verify` into `docs/verification.raw.json`. The verification is pinned to block `71,272,554` (`0x43f886a`) until this document is deliberately refreshed.

## Verified

- X Layer mainnet reports chain ID `196` through the official public RPC.
- At the pinned block, the base fee was `20,000,000 wei` (`0.02 gwei`). The observed RPC gas price was `20,000,001 wei`.
- Bytecode exists at the canonical ERC-4337 EntryPoint v0.6, v0.7, and v0.8 addresses. This proves deployment, not provider support; the product must select one exact version with its account provider.
- The issuer API identifies all three supplied wrapper addresses as X Layer `wrapperAddressV2` deployments. Their on-chain `asset()` values match the issuer's native underlying addresses and each wrapper reports 18 decimals.
- X Layer uses EVM contracts. Solana Token-2022 does not apply to these deployments; xStocks implements multiplier/rebasing behavior through its EVM wrapper design.
- USDT0 is `0x779ded0c9e1022225f8e0630b35a9b54be713736` and reports 6 decimals.
- Uniswap V3 factory and QuoterV2 addresses are sourced from Uniswap's X Layer deployment registry. Executable, pinned-block quotes are recorded in the raw result.

## Not yet verified / blockers

- No bundler, paymaster, or exact smart-account factory has been selected and verified.
- No funded deployer/provider credentials are present, so a real counterfactual smart-account deployment and sponsored UserOperation cannot be submitted or recorded.
- Session-key support, paymaster pre-charge/refund behavior, ERC-20 payment mode, EntryPoint deposit/stake funding, and relayer-private routing remain unverified provider capabilities.
- Gas-price variance needs repeated observations over time; one block only confirms the floor at that instant.
- The issuer API and RPC evidence must be refreshed at deploy time because wrapper certification, trading halts, multipliers, and liquidity can change.

These are hard gate failures under the engineering specification. Contract implementation must not begin until a provider is chosen and one sponsored mainnet UserOperation succeeds.
